About & contact

Questions, feedback or a wedding for 200 people? We read every message.

Started in a dorm kitchen, now baking for the city

PizzaGo began with a simple problem: students wanted good pizza fast, without calling anyone. We built a site where you choose, pay and track in a couple of minutes.

Our dough rests for 48 hours, the mozzarella comes from a local dairy and every pizza goes into the oven only after you order. That is why it tastes the way it does.
